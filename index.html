Block 001

What the SIG does

06 entries
01

Advance research

Sponsor tracks, mini-tracks, and paper sessions at AIS conferences so blockchain work is reviewed by people who understand it.

02

Convene the community

Workshops, webinars, and meet-ups where faculty, doctoral students, and practitioners argue about the same problems in the same room.

03

Share knowledge

Reading lists, datasets, syllabi, and tooling, curated so nobody has to build a blockchain course or study from a blank page.

04

Support new scholars

Doctoral consortia, mentoring, and reviewer development that give early-career researchers a route into the field and into review.

05

Bridge to practice

Bring builders, protocol teams, and policy makers into the conversation so the research questions track what the technology is actually doing.

06

Inform policy

Translate peer-reviewed findings into evidence that regulators, standards bodies, and institutions can use.

The questions we care about

Blockchain is not one research problem. It is a stack of them — cryptographic, economic, organizational, and institutional — and information systems research sits where they meet.

  • Consensus & scalability
  • Smart contracts

  • Decentralized finance
  • Tokenomics & incentive design
  • Stablecoins & CBDCs
  • Market microstructure
  • NFTs & digital ownership
  • Creator economies
  • Web3.0 applications
  • DAOs & decentralized governance
  • Enterprise adoption
  • Supply chain & traceability
  • Self-sovereign identity
