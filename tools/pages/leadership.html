Block 001

What the officers do

Officer work is practical: it keeps events on the calendar, submissions reviewed, and the membership informed.

06 roles
01

President

Sets direction for the SIG, represents it within AIS, and chairs the officer team.

02

Vice President

Supports the president, leads membership growth, and steps in when needed.

03

Secretary

Keeps records, minutes, and the membership roster, and runs elections.

04

Treasurer

Manages SIG finances and reporting in coordination with AIS.

05

Program Chair

Coordinates sponsored tracks, workshops, and the webinar series.

06

Communications Chair

Runs the newsletter, this website, and the SIG's social channels.
