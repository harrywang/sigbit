Record 0005/Membership/Open to researchers and practitioners

Join
SIGBIT

Membership is open to anyone with an interest in blockchain-driven innovations and technologies. Three steps, none of them long.

Block 001

How to join

03 steps
01

Become an AIS member

SIGs are communities within the Association for Information Systems. Membership brings the eLibrary, conference rates, and the SIG network. Join AIS

02

Add SIGBIT to your profile

Select the Special Interest Group on Blockchain Innovations and Technologies in your AIS member profile, or via the SIG directory.

03

Join the mailing list

The list is how calls for papers, webinar invitations, and event announcements reach you. Sign up below.

Block 002

What members get

04 entries
01

Calls and deadlines, first

Sponsored tracks, special issues, and workshop calls reach the list before they circulate widely.

02

A room for work in progress

The webinar series and workshops are built for papers that are not finished yet.

03

Reviewers and collaborators

People who actually work on blockchain — useful for co-authors, reviewers, and PhD committees.

04

A path to service

Reviewer, track chair, and officer roles that count toward your service record.

Block 003

Mailing list

Occasional email: calls for papers, event announcements, and a short digest of what the community is publishing. Unsubscribe any time.

Not yet wired

Sign-up is not connected to a mail service yet. Until it is, use the contact routes below and we will add you to the list by hand.

Block 004

Reach the officer team

Membership questions, event and special-issue proposals, corrections to this site, or an offer to volunteer.

03 routes
Route 01

Email

The SIG's contact address is being finalized. Until then, reach the officers through the AIS SIG directory.

Route 02

Through AIS

For membership records, dues, and account questions, AIS staff are the right first stop. aisnet.org

Route 03

This website

The site is static and open. Corrections and additions can go to the communications chair or straight to the repository.

For maintainers Add the SIG email address, social accounts, and the mailing-list endpoint in tools/pages/join.html, then re-run ./tools/build.sh.
